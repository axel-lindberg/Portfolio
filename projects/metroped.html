More realism

Better lighting, assets, spatial audio and crowd rendering.

MetroPed is a virtual reality simulation of Stockholm's metro system developed in collaboration with ESAL at KTH Royal Institute of Technology. The project supports research into how people perceive safety and behave in public transport environments by creating an immersive VR simulation of a metro station.

The project focused on increasing the realism and immersion of the simulation by improving visual fidelity, lighting, environmental assets, spatial audio, and crowd rendering.
