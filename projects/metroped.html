Overview

A virtual reality simulation of Stockholm's metro, built to help researchers study how people perceive safety and behave in public transport.

MetroPed was developed in collaboration with ESAL at KTH Royal Institute of Technology. Our work focused on making the simulation more realistic and immersive by improving visual fidelity, lighting, environmental assets, spatial audio and crowd rendering. My role

Project management

Led a team of six developers as Scrum Master, managing sprints and project risks with Agile and Scrum, and delivered a client-validated MVP ahead of schedule.

Impostor rendering

Engineered a custom impostor rendering pipeline in Unity and C#, reducing per-character draw calls by 50% and frame time by 32% in desktop benchmarks without compromising visual fidelity, enabling larger and more realistic crowd simulations.

Git & documentation

Designed the team's Git workflow and maintained thorough documentation to keep the project scalable and make the handover to stakeholders seamless.
